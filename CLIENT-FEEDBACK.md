# Client Feedback

## Client

Reid Begelman

## Feedback Round

After sharing the deployed first draft of the portfolio website with Reid, I asked him to review the site and provide feedback on the content, layout, and overall presentation.

## Client Feedback

Reid said:

> Overall, I think it’s a really good first draft. If I were to make some edits to it I think that from a more editorial sense I would sort of shift from being hired to being someone that could be employed. From a career standpoint I’m also more focused on the strategic portion than marketing so having that be first in the order would be ideal. Then, in the sense of design, I think that the head shot could maybe be smaller and less of a focal point. Look forward to seeing the final site.

## Changes Requested

Based on Reid's feedback, I identified three changes:

1. Adjust the wording so the website presents Reid as a professional candidate rather than directly encouraging visitors to hire him.
2. Make Strategy the first professional focus area because Reid is more interested in strategy than marketing.
3. Reduce the size and visual prominence of Reid's headshot.

## Planned Changes

- Revise any language that feels too focused on being hired.
- Change the focus order to:
  1. Strategy
  2. Consulting
  3. Marketing
- Reduce the size of the headshot while keeping it visible in the homepage introduction.

## Purpose of the Revision

These changes will make the website better reflect Reid's actual career priorities and the way he wants to present himself professionally.
