# Mini Project 1 Proposal

## Client Brief

**Client:** Reid Begelman, a senior at Babson College pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration with dual concentrations in Marketing and Strategy and Consulting.

**Purpose:** Create a professional personal portfolio website that introduces Reid, presents his academic and professional background, makes his resume easy to access, and gives visitors clear ways to contact him.

**Audience:** The primary audience is recruiters, potential employers, professional contacts, academic contacts, and anyone who wants to learn more about Reid's background and experience.

**Key action:** The main action I want a visitor to take is to view Reid's resume. A secondary action is to contact him through email, phone, or LinkedIn.

---

## Style Preferences

Reid wanted the site to function as a professional portfolio rather than simply placing his resume on a webpage.

He also wanted the three pages to share the same layout and visual identity.

The design should therefore feel:

- Professional
- Modern
- Clean
- Consistent
- Easy to navigate
- Appropriate for employers and recruiters
- Personal enough to represent Reid rather than looking like a generic template

The photograph and professional information should be prominent, while the Resume and Contact pages should be easy to reach from anywhere on the site.

For the visual system, I chose:

- Dark navy as the primary color
- Warm cream as the background
- Warm orange as the accent
- Large typography for Reid's name
- A prominent professional photograph
- Numbered sections for visual organization
- Limited decorative elements
- Strong spacing and hierarchy

My goal is to make the site feel like a professional editorial portfolio rather than a generic AI-generated website.

## Inspiration Site

### Ameer Hamza

**Site:** https://ameerhamzastrategist.com/

I used Ameer Hamza's personal website as the main inspiration for the direction of Reid's portfolio.

What stood out to me was how quickly the website communicates who the person is and what he does. The homepage puts the person's professional identity, photograph, positioning, and important actions near the top rather than making the visitor search for them.

I also liked the way the website is organized into clear sections. The content feels professional and personal at the same time.

### What I Took From This Site

I wanted to use several ideas from this type of personal-brand website:

- A strong introduction at the top of the homepage
- Clear professional positioning
- A prominent photograph
- Strong visual hierarchy
- Clear calls to action
- Professional information that is easy to scan
- A consistent identity across the entire website

### How I Applied It to Reid's Website

Reid's homepage places his name, professional focus, short introduction, photograph, Resume button, and LinkedIn link in the first section.

The goal is for a recruiter to understand within a few seconds that Reid is a Babson College senior focused on Marketing, Strategy, and Consulting.

The rest of the homepage is divided into clearly labeled sections:

- Profile
- Areas of Interest
- Experience

This keeps Reid's professional story easy to follow without copying the structure or branding of the inspiration site.

### What I Did Not Copy

I did not copy the site's exact visual design, wording, services, or layout.

Ameer Hamza's website is designed around his work as a strategist and personal brand, while Reid's website has a different purpose. Reid needs a student and early-career professional portfolio centered around:

- Education
- Resume
- Internship experience
- LinkedIn
- Contact information
- Future employment

The inspiration was therefore used for overall direction rather than as a template.

## Responsive Layout

The site needs to work on both desktop and mobile devices.

On smaller screens:

- The two-column homepage hero becomes one column
- Reid's photograph moves below the introductory content
- The three Areas of Interest cards stack vertically
- The three Contact sections stack vertically
- Font sizes become smaller
- Navigation remains readable
- Images resize to fit the screen
- Text stays inside the viewport
- No horizontal scrolling should appear

## Starting Prompt Description

The following description was used as the starting direction for AI:

> Build a three-page professional portfolio website for Reid Begelman, a senior at Babson College pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Business Administration with concentrations in Marketing and Strategy and Consulting.
>
> The website should include About, Resume, and Contact pages with the same navigation on every page.
>
> The homepage should use a professional editorial-style layout rather than a generic card-heavy portfolio. Place Reid's name, professional focus, short introduction, Resume button, and LinkedIn link beside a large professional photograph.
>
> Below the first section, include clearly organized sections for Profile, Areas of Interest, and Experience. The Areas of Interest section should cover Marketing, Strategy, and Consulting. The Experience section should show a simple progression from Babson College to Reid's 2026 internship at Restaurant Brands International and his full-time return beginning in July 2027.
>
> The Resume page should include a direct PDF link and an embedded resume viewer.
>
> The Contact page should clearly show Reid's phone number, email address, and LinkedIn profile.
>
> Use semantic HTML including header, nav, main, section, article, and footer. Use one external CSS stylesheet shared by every page. Build the layouts using CSS Grid and Flexbox and make the site responsive on mobile devices.
>
> Use a restrained professional color system based on dark navy, warm cream, and orange. Avoid excessive gradients, unnecessary effects, and generic AI portfolio styling.
