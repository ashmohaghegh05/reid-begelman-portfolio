## Required Question

### Pick one piece of AI output you did not accept as-is. What did it give you, what did you change, and how did you know it needed changing?

The first version of the AI-generated Experience section was too limited. It mainly showed Babson College, Reid's RBI internship, and his future full-time role.

After receiving feedback from Reid and from the class showcase, I reviewed his actual resume and expanded the Experience section to include more of his professional and leadership background, including Babson Consulting Association, TAMID at Babson, SSG Capital Advisors, Premium Minds, and Plantopia Bio.

I knew the original version needed changing because Reid specifically asked for more of his experiences and e-board positions to appear under the `03 / EXPERIENCE` section. The showcase feedback also repeatedly suggested adding more detail so employers could learn more about him.
